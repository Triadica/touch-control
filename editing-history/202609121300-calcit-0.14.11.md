# Calcit 0.14.11 / Calcit 0.14.11

## 中文

- 将 Calcit 从 0.14.7 升级到 0.14.11，保持触摸/指针与 DOM 宿主契约及模块版本不变。
- 结构化的 `Fn` schema 取代整份 `Dynamic`；把返回宿主对象的函数 schema 改为限定 trait 类型 `touch-control.core/DomElementHost`，消除返回类型不匹配告警。
- CI 固定 `actions/checkout`、`actions/setup-node`、`calcit-lang/setup-calcit` 到不可变 commit。

## English

- Upgrade Calcit from 0.14.7 to 0.14.11, preserving the touch/pointer and DOM-host contracts and module version.
- Replace whole-`Dynamic` schemas with structured `Fn`; type host-returning functions with the qualified trait `touch-control.core/DomElementHost` to clear the return-type mismatch warnings.
- Pin `actions/checkout`, `actions/setup-node`, and `calcit-lang/setup-calcit` in CI to immutable commits.
